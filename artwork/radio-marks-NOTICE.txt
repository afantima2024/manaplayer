ManaMusicRadio station-mark artwork

The eleven radio-program emblems (news, talk, pop, classic, piano, acoustic,
jazz, world, ambient, folk and dance) are original artwork generated for
this project by scripts\Generate-RadioMarks.py. Each uses an FM receiver,
antenna and tuning dial with a distinct program symbol; these are not
logos of actual broadcasters. Existing saved mark identifiers are retained.

Button themes
scripts\Generate-AppearanceAssets.py generates the shared day/night
gradients. Logo gradient follows the colors of the supplied application
mark. Copilot aurora and Copilot sunrise are original, Copilot-inspired
color palettes on this application's own button shapes, not copies of
the Copilot logo or a claim of endorsement.

Radio station identification image sources

These image addresses identify broadcasts from China National Radio / CMG.
The logos and station marks belong to their respective broadcaster. Their use
does not imply affiliation or endorsement. They are not original app artwork.

Source: the public CNR web-player catalogue, POST https://apppc.cnr.cn/national
Field: other_info6. Retrieved 2026-10-31. No station images are bundled.
Only the selected, playing station may download a logo when enabled. Downloads
are bounded to 512 KiB, decoded and resized to at most 128 pixels, and cached
as PNG in private app files until the user clears radio logos. Imported M3U
tvg-logo addresses follow the same policy.
